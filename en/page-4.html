About Us

ISO9001 Quality Management

The company is certified to ISO9001. Documented procedures and audits cover incoming inspection, in-process parameter control and final inspection, supported by AOI automated inspection and a performance testing lab so every batch of labels, nameplates and insulation sheets stays consistent.

Guided by Innovation, Excellence and Win-win Cooperation, we run total quality management across the whole process: artwork and colour separation, die tooling, film output, colour matching and printing, screen making, die cutting and forming — all inside cleanrooms. Roll products receive 100% AOI inspection, backed by tensile, initial tack, holding power, colour difference, gloss, abrasion and 2.5D measurement testing to keep every batch consistent.
