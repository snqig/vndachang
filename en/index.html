ABOUT US
About Us
Established in 2024 with registered capital of USD 6 million and a growing team of 40, Vietnam Dachang Printing Tech Co., Ltd. is located at Lot CN-02, Viet-Nhat Industrial Cluster, Xuan Cam Commune, Bac Ninh Province. We manufacture battery labels, PC nameplates, adhesive stickers and insulation sheets, and are certified to ISO9001, ISO14001 and QC080000 with more than 60 UL certifications……
